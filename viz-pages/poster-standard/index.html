The mechanism was there, in pieces

The posters were good and getting better, and the dives were freeform, which was fine while there were four of them. At thirty-eight it stopped being fine in one specific way: the architecture never got a section.

It was there — but spread across asides. voicemode-hold explained its mechanism in four separate places: "Getting it back to the agent", "Why it writes the socket directly", "How the pane behaves", and a stepper under "Watch a hold". Every one of those was worth reading. None of them let you follow a single utterance from the tool call to the words arriving back in the agent's context.

Two other things fell out of the same freedom: three owned things had no poster at all, and no poster said where its tool kept state, even though every tool had been declaring exactly that in its frontmatter since the manifest landed.

Marking a section

One attribute. The heading is yours.

<h2 data-beat="why">The predecessor died and nobody noticed</h2>
<h2 data-beat="using">The two keys</h2>
<h2 data-beat="limits">It reports. It never fixes.</h2>
<h2 data-beat="where">Where it lives</h2>
<h2 data-beat="how">Cracking it open</h2>

Beats may be split — three sections can all carry data-beat="how" — but they may not interleave. Order is fixed: why, using, limits, where, how.

Limits sit third on purpose. Someone still deciding whether to care deserves the ceilings before the internals, not after four screens of mechanism.

What the standard refuses to do

  • It has no opinion about the card. The 1200×630 hero is the part that has to earn a click, and a rule there would produce forty identical cards.
  • It never reads heading text. That is the whole design. It cannot therefore tell a vivid heading from a lazy one.
  • It cannot tell whether the how beat is any good. It checks that a diagram or stepper exists and that the section is present. Whether the explanation is true is a reading job, and this repo has reviewers for that.
  • It does not touch prose quality anywhere. A poster can pass every check and still be boring. Structure is a floor, not a ceiling.

The specviz-pages/POSTER-STANDARD.md — the normative copy, for whoever writes the next poster.

What it readsThe rascaltwo-ai-setup: frontmatter in every tools/*/README.md and skills/*/SKILL.md, and the data-beat attributes in each poster.
Its stateNone. It is a one-shot report that reads the tree and exits. Nothing to clean up, nothing under ~/.agents/state/.

The inventory comes from the manifests, not a list

There is no registry of what this repo owns. The checker walks tools/*/README.md and skills/*/SKILL.md, and anything whose frontmatter carries a rascaltwo-ai-setup: block is an owned thing. That is deliberate: a hand-maintained list is a list that goes stale the first time someone adds a skill in a hurry, and the failure mode is silent — the missing poster simply never gets reported.

Slug resolution, and why it tries three

A poster for ttyimgspool lives at viz-pages/tool-ttyimgspool/, one for dream at viz-pages/skill-dream/. The checker tries <kind>-<name> first, then both prefixes, so a skill that grew into a tool does not silently lose its poster the moment its manifest kind changes.

Order is checked on first appearance

Beats are allowed to repeat, which means "in order" needs defining. The rule is the first occurrence of each beat: collect the index of the first data-beat="why", the first "using", and so on, then assert that sequence is ascending. Three how sections in a row are fine. A why section after them is not.

The frontmatter cross-check

This is the only check with an opinion about content, and it exists because two descriptions of one fact always drift. A tool declaring state: true whose poster never names ~/.agents/state/<name>/ fails. So does the reverse — a poster describing state the manifest says the thing does not keep.

A state-owner is exempt. r2-gauntlet writes into r2-sdlc's store on purpose, so it has no directory of its own and naming one would be the lie, not the omission.

The visual check is deliberately crude

Beat 5 must carry a diagram or a stepper, and the test for that is whether the page contains an <svg>, a call to stepper(, or a class="fig". That is a blunt instrument and it is supposed to be: it catches "this beat is four paragraphs and nothing else", which was the real failure, and it does not pretend to judge whether the diagram helps.
