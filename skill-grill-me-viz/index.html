↓  WHY IT EXISTS

Words are a bad way to ask about a screen

A terminal interview can only describe a screen in sentences: "tabs across the top, or a sidebar?" You answer from imagination, the agent builds from your imagined answer, and three hours later you meet the real thing and it is wrong. The cheapest moment to be wrong was the question. So the question carries the thing itself: you look at it, play with it, and answer with a click, or with your voice while you point.

grill-me-viz keeps the discipline of grilling (one question at a time, a recommendation with its reason, walk the decision tree) and adds the medium: a page that stays open for the whole session, hot-reloads as the agent writes it, and records what you click and what you say. It was built to be used heavily, and it has been:

Say it, and a page opens

Triggers: "grill me visually", "grill me viz" (or "grill me vis"), or any grilling question that can't be settled in words: how a screen looks, one layout or three. The standing rule is in AGENTS.md §3: before finalizing a plan, run grill-me-viz, and plain grilling only where viz can't run.

  • You get one URL. The tab stays open all session and hot-reloads as the agent adds each round. If the tab isn't focused, the favicon turns orange and the title reads "● Your turn".
  • Click an option. Picking highlights it, then folds the question to one line; clicking the line reopens it.
  • Or talk while you point. 🎙 in the corner starts the mic; each stretch of speech is anchored to whatever the pointer is over, with a picture of the page as you began and as you stopped. Alt-click types a line instead. 💬 shows everything you've said and picked; click a picture to see it full size.
  • Send round N when you're done. Answering in the terminal still works and wins for the same question.
  • Grill someone else. Export the page to one file and send it (Slack works). They answer in their own browser with nothing to install; their answers stay in that browser until they press Copy in the 💬 panel, which hands back every word and a numbered picture of each moment they pointed at.

When A, B, C and D aren't enough, just say so

Options are a floor, not a ceiling. If none of the choices is what you mean, you don't have to pick the least-wrong letter: start the mic, point at the thing you mean, and talk. It asks. It does not decide.

grill-me-viz only asks the questions and records your answers; the call is always yours, and a recommendation is just a recommendation. It leans on the viz skill: with viz's live server the answers reach the agent directly; from an exported file they wait in that browser until someone copies them out.

Where it lives and what it keeps

The skill skills/grill-me-viz/: one SKILL.md with the seven steps, and a small page script and stylesheet that get copied beside each new page.
Declared kind: skill, state: false. requires.skills: [grilling, viz], the two it combines. No integrates, no deletion-policy.
Its state None of its own. The skill keeps nothing between runs. A session's answers live in the page it made, in the viz library under grill-<topic>/, beside a log of every click and spoken line (git-ignored, never published). That log is viz's state, not this skill's, and it is the only record of the spoken half of a grill: delete it and the picks survive as the page's decided lines, the spoken reactions do not. Answered from an exported file, a session lives in that person's browser instead, until they copy it out.
Requires Step 1 loads two skills: grilling, then viz, in its full mode. The mic transcribes on your device (Chrome's own recognizer, or Parakeet if you take the download); typed answers need nothing beyond viz itself. Someone answering an exported page needs only a browser (Chrome, for the mic).
Called from AGENTS.md §3 (Planning & Verification): "Grill visually: before finalizing a plan, run grill-me-viz." It is a standing rule rather than something the model is trusted to reach for. It also builds on viz's feedback widget, which owns the pill, the mic and the log.

How a round travels

One question goes from the agent to your click, into a log file, and back as a rewritten page. Everything you do on the page lands as a line in a log beside it; the agent waits for your Send, reads the log, and turns your answers into decisions.

Run the machine yourself

A real round of a visual grill, with the real feedback widget. Pick an option, or click 🎙 in the demo's corner and talk while you point at the option you mean; Alt/Option-click anything to type instead. Watch what the agent would read fill the black box, pictures included, then press Send and play the agent. Speech is turned into text on your device by the browser (or by Parakeet, the skill's own model, if you take the download); nothing you say or type leaves this page, and nothing is saved.
